Technical Service FAQs

Why is my tankless water heater displaying error code 11 during a freeze in Houston?

Error Code 11 (common on Rinnai and Navien units) indicates ignition failure. During sudden Houston freezes, gas regulators at the meter can occasionally freeze or lock up due to moisture in the municipal line, dropping the dynamic gas pressure. Alternatively, the exhaust vent may be blocked by frost. We highly recommend insulated intake/exhaust runs and checking the gas meter regulator before assuming the heat exchanger has failed.

Do I need to upgrade my gas line to a 3/4-inch pipe to install a Navien NPE-240A2 in Cypress?

It depends on the distance from your gas meter and the total BTU load of your home. The Navien NPE-240A2 uses dual stainless steel heat exchangers and allows for 1/2-inch gas lines up to 24 feet (under optimal pressure). However, if your Cypress home has multiple gas appliances (furnace, stove, dryer) running simultaneously, we often recommend upgrading the trunk line to 3/4-inch or 1-inch to ensure a static pressure of at least 3.5" WC during peak winter demands.

How does the notoriously hard water in Montgomery County affect my heat exchanger?

Montgomery County relies heavily on aquifer groundwater, resulting in calcium carbonate levels often exceeding 15 grains per gallon (gpg). This causes rapid scale buildup inside the narrow copper or stainless steel tubing of a tankless heat exchanger. Without an inline scale inhibitor (like a TAC filter) or an annual descaling flush using food-grade white vinegar, the scale acts as an insulator. This forces the burner to run hotter, dramatically reducing the lifespan of the unit from 20 years to under 7 years.

If my PEX-A pipes freeze in Katy, will they burst like PVC or copper?

PEX-A (cross-linked polyethylene) is highly resilient and can expand up to 30% of its diameter before rupturing, meaning it is far less likely to burst during a Katy freeze than rigid copper or CPVC. However, the brass or poly-alloy fittings connecting the PEX lines do not expand. If water freezes solid exactly at a fitting, the joint can fail. This is why we still mandate complete exterior insulation on all exposed PEX-A manifolds and runs in unconditioned attics.
