Expert Insights

COMMON TOILET & SHOWER PROBLEMS

Maybe you’ve already experienced what it’s like to be in the middle of a plumbing crisis and not know who to call for help. Have Liberty Plumbing on speed dial for when you end up with unexpected issues like:

Sluggish Toilets

When you flush your toilet and it seems weak or slow, there's a good chance there's a problem in the trap or drain line.

Rocking Toilets

A toilet that rocks from side to side needs to be stabilized and reseated immediately before you end up with leaks that rot the subfloor.

Whistling Sounds

Whistling or hissing from the toilet tank is an indication that the fill valve is faulty and wasting water constantly.

Leaking Shower Pans

If water is dripping into the room below your bathroom, your shower pan or drain seal has failed and requires urgent replacement.
