General Plumbing
FAQs.

Expert answers to the most common residential plumbing emergencies in the Greater Houston area.

What should I do immediately if a pipe bursts in my house?

The absolute first thing you must do is locate your main water shut-off valve and turn it off to stop the flooding. This valve is typically located near your water meter at the street, in your garage, or on an exterior wall. Once the water is off, open your faucets to drain the remaining water in the lines, and then call our 24/7 emergency dispatch immediately to repair the broken section.

How do I know if my underground main sewer line is collapsed or just clogged?

If multiple drains in your home (toilets, showers, sinks) are backing up simultaneously, the issue is definitely in the main sewer line. A simple clog can usually be cleared with professional hydro-jetting or an industrial snake. However, if the line repeatedly clogs shortly after being cleared, or if you notice sinkholes or extremely lush patches of grass in your yard, the pipe may have collapsed due to root intrusion or ground shifting. We use high-definition sewer cameras to visually inspect the line and confirm the exact cause without guessing.

Why does my toilet constantly run, and how much is it costing me?

A constantly running toilet is usually caused by a deteriorated flapper valve, an improperly adjusted float, or a faulty fill valve. While it sounds like a minor annoyance, a continuously running toilet can waste up to 200 gallons of water per day, easily adding $50 to $100+ to your monthly water bill. Our technicians can replace the internal tank mechanisms in minutes, paying for itself in utility savings almost instantly.

What are the signs of a hidden slab leak?

Slab leaks occur when a pressurized water line running under your home's concrete foundation bursts or develops a pinhole leak. Key signs include: a sudden unexplained spike in your water bill, the sound of running water when all fixtures are turned off, unexplained warm spots on your floor, damp carpets, or low water pressure. If you suspect a slab leak, we deploy advanced acoustic leak detection equipment to pinpoint the exact location through the concrete before drilling.

Is it safe to use chemical drain cleaners from the hardware store?

We strongly advise against using harsh chemical drain cleaners (like Drano or Liquid Plumr). These chemicals rely on caustic reactions that generate intense heat to melt clogs. In older homes with cast iron or PVC pipes, this heat can permanently warp, crack, or corrode the pipes, turning a simple $150 clog removal into a $2,000 repiping job. Professional snaking or hydro-jetting safely removes the blockage without damaging your home's infrastructure.
